# CHOps Corporate Contributor License Agreement

**Version 1.0**

Thank you for your organisation's interest in contributing to CHOps.

This Agreement covers contributions to CHOps made by employees of a company,
rather than by an individual acting for themselves.

If your employees contribute to CHOps as part of their work, their employer
almost certainly owns the copyright in what they produce. In that case an
individual agreement signed by the employee does not grant us anything, because
the employee is not the owner. This Agreement fixes that once, for everyone at
your organisation, instead of asking each person to make a promise they cannot
keep.

The terms mirror the individual agreement in [CLA.md](CLA.md). Nothing here is
broader.

To put this in place, fill in the schedule at the end, have it signed by someone
authorised to bind the company, and send it to **chops@quantrail-data.com**. We
will countersign and return a copy.

In consideration of the mutual promises below, and of Us reviewing Your
Contributors' work and, where We accept it, maintaining and distributing it as
part of CHOps, the parties agree as follows.

---

## 1. What the words mean

**"You"** means the legal entity named in the schedule, together with all
entities that control, are controlled by, or are under common control with it,
where control means beneficial ownership of more than fifty percent of the
voting securities or the power to direct management.

**"Us"**, **"We"**, and **"Our"** mean Quantrail Data Private Limited together
with its Affiliates, successors in interest, and permitted assigns.
**"Affiliate"** carries the same meaning as in CLA.md.

**"Contributor"** means an employee, officer, or contractor of Yours who
submits a Contribution to Us, and whom You have designated under section 2.

**"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship, including any
modification of or addition to an existing work, intentionally submitted by a
Contributor to Us for inclusion in any product We own or manage (the "Work").
"Submitted" means any electronic, verbal, or written communication sent to Us or
Our representatives, including mailing lists, source control, and issue trackers
run by or for Us for the purpose of discussing and improving the Work. It
excludes communication conspicuously marked in writing as "Not a Contribution"
at the time it is submitted.

**"Feedback"** means suggestions, feature requests, bug reports, ideas,
know-how, and comments provided to Us about the Work, other than Contributions.

## 2. Who this covers

This Agreement covers Contributions from the people listed in the schedule, and
from anyone You add later by writing to **chops@quantrail-data.com**.

Keeping that list current is Your responsibility. A Contribution from someone
You have not designated is not covered here, and We will ask that person to
accept the individual agreement instead.

You may remove a person at any time by writing to Us. Removal applies to
Contributions submitted afterwards. It does not affect anything already
submitted, and the permissions granted for those Contributions continue.

## 3. Scope and application

This Agreement applies to every Contribution Your Contributors submitted before
You signed it, and to every Contribution they submit afterwards. For work
submitted before signature, the permissions in sections 5 and 6 apply as of the
date it was first submitted.

Where a Contributor previously accepted the individual agreement for work that
You in fact own, this Agreement governs that work.

## 4. You keep your copyright

You keep all right, title, and interest in Your Contributions. Nothing here
transfers ownership to Us.

You remain free to use, license, and distribute Your Contributions as submitted,
standing alone, however You like, to anyone, on any terms. This section gives You
no claim over the Work as a whole, over another contributor's code, or over a
combined or derivative work We build.

## 5. The permission you give us

You grant Us and recipients of software We distribute a perpetual, worldwide,
non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright licence to
reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform,
sublicense, and distribute Your Contributions and those derivative works.

**This includes the right to distribute Your Contributions, and derivative works
of them, under any licence terms We choose. Those terms include Our commercial
licence for the CHOps core and the proprietary licence covering CHOps Pro.
Neither of those requires Us to publish source code.**

Nothing obliges Us to use Your Contribution in any product.

## 6. Patents

You grant Us and recipients of software We distribute a perpetual, worldwide,
non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable (except as below) patent
licence to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import, and otherwise
transfer the Work.

This reaches only patent claims You can license that are necessarily infringed by
a Contribution on its own, or by combining that Contribution with the Work it was
submitted to.

If any entity brings patent litigation, including a cross-claim or counterclaim,
alleging that a Contribution or the Work infringes a patent, every patent licence
granted to that entity under this Agreement ends on the day that case is filed.

## 7. Ideas and feedback

Feedback is voluntary and not confidential. We may use, disclose, and
commercially exploit it without restriction, attribution, or payment, and We are
not obliged to act on it.

You get no stake in any feature, product, or improvement We build that relates to
Your Feedback, including anything in CHOps Pro.

## 8. Moral rights

So far as the law allows, You will procure that Your Contributors do not assert
against Us, Our licensees, or Our customers any moral rights or rights of
paternity or integrity in Your Contributions, including rights under section 57
of the Copyright Act, 1957 (India) and equivalent provisions elsewhere.

Where those rights cannot be waived, You will procure that they are not exercised
in a way that stops Us using the permissions in sections 5 and 6.

## 9. What you are confirming

**(a) Authority.** The person signing below is authorised to bind You, and You
are legally entitled to grant the permissions in this Agreement.

**(b) Ownership.** You own or otherwise control the rights in the Contributions
Your Contributors submit, whether by employment agreement, contractor assignment,
or otherwise, sufficiently to grant everything in sections 5 and 6.

**(c) Originality.** Each Contribution is the original creation of the
Contributor who submitted it.

**(d) Third-party material.** Contributions may include material that is not
originally Yours only if all four of the following hold:

  1. It is identified clearly at the time of submission;
  2. Its source is disclosed, with any licence, patent, trademark, or other
     restriction You are aware of;
  3. It is marked `Submitted on behalf of a third party: [name]`; and
  4. Its licence lets Us exercise everything in sections 5 and 6, sublicensing
     under proprietary terms included, or We have agreed in writing beforehand
     to take it anyway.

Point 4 is the one that matters most. Code under a copyleft licence such as the
GPL or AGPL does not meet it, and disclosing its origin does not change that. If
You are unsure, ask before Your team builds around it.

**(e) Notification.** You will tell Us promptly if You learn that anything
confirmed in this section is no longer true.

## 10. No warranty, no support obligation

Unless the law requires otherwise or We agree in writing, Contributions are
provided on an "AS IS" BASIS, WITHOUT WARRANTIES OR CONDITIONS OF ANY KIND,
express or implied, including any warranty of TITLE, NON-INFRINGEMENT,
MERCHANTABILITY, or FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

You are not expected to support Your Contributions.

## 11. No confidentiality

Contributions and Feedback are submitted publicly and are not confidential. We
owe You no duty of confidentiality over anything submitted, so do not send
anything that has to stay secret. Security reports are the exception, and have
their own private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## 12. No trademark licence

This Agreement gives You no right to use Our names, logos, or trademarks,
including CHOps® and Quantrail™, beyond ordinary attribution when describing
where the Work came from.

## 13. Term and survival

This Agreement runs until terminated. Either of us may end it with thirty days'
written notice, which stops it covering Contributions submitted after that date.

Ending it does not affect permissions already granted for Contributions already
submitted. Sections 4 to 8 and 10 to 14 continue to apply.

## 14. Other terms

**Assignment.** We may transfer this Agreement, whole or in part, without Your
consent, including as part of a merger, acquisition, reorganisation, or sale of
assets. You may not transfer it without Our written consent, except to a
successor to substantially all of Your business.

**Governing law.** Indian law governs this Agreement, without regard to conflict
of law provisions. The courts at Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India have exclusive
jurisdiction.

**Entire agreement.** This is the whole agreement between us about Contributions
by Your Contributors, and replaces anything agreed earlier on the subject,
including any individual agreement they accepted covering the same work. It
changes only in writing signed by both parties.

**Severability.** If any part of this is unenforceable, it is narrowed just
enough to work, and the rest stands.
